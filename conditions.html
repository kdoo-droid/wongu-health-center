Conditions We Treat

What patients come to Wongu Health Center for, and an honest picture of where acupuncture and Chinese medicine can help. We always work alongside your medical care, not in place of it.

Condition guides

These are the concerns we see most often. Each guide explains how we treat it, how many visits to expect, and warning signs that mean you should see a doctor first. We always work alongside your medical care, not in place of it.

Back PainChronic and recurring low back pain. Acupuncture is among the non-drug options recommended by the American College of Physicians. Neck & Shoulder PainStiffness and tension from desk work, driving, and stress. SciaticaShooting leg pain from an irritated sciatic nerve, often due to a herniated disc. Knee & Joint PainKnee pain, arthritis, and joint stiffness. Headaches & MigrainesPreventive care for migraine and frequent tension-type headaches. Stress & AnxietySupportive care for stress and anxious feelings. Sleep & InsomniaSupportive care for trouble sleeping, including for shift workers. Women's HealthMenstrual concerns, fertility, pregnancy, and menopause. Digestive HealthBloating, indigestion, and digestive comfort. Nausea & Treatment SupportSupport after surgery and during cancer care.

What we don't treat

Acupuncture is not emergency care, and it is not a substitute for diagnosis by a physician. If you have chest pain, signs of a stroke, a sudden severe headache, a high fever, or any other emergency, call 911. If you have a new, worsening, or unexplained symptom, see your doctor first. We are happy to see you once serious causes have been ruled out.

Not sure if we can help?

Call us at (702) 852-1280 and tell the front desk what's going on. They can help you decide whether to book with a supervised intern or a licensed OMD. Or read what to expect at your first visit.

Book your first visit

First visits from $80. Self-pay patients can book online. VA Community Care and Culinary Health Fund patients should contact Wongu Health Center before scheduling so we can verify eligibility, authorization, and provider availability.
