Package terms

  • What's included: 5 or 10 basic acupuncture follow-up visits with a licensed OMD. Packages don't include the initial visit. Herbal formulas and powders are extra. Anything beyond basic acupuncture, such as cupping, is up to your OMD.
  • Who can buy one: self-pay patients who have completed their initial visit. Packages can't be used with insurance, including VA Community Care and Culinary Health Fund, or with a superbill for insurance reimbursement. Each package belongs to one patient's account and can't be shared or moved to another account.
  • Payment: paid in full when you buy the package.
  • Time limit: use your visits within 3 months of purchase for a 5-visit package, or 6 months for a 10-visit package.
  • Community discount packages: only for seniors (65+), military and veterans, non-Wongu students, minors and pediatric patients, people with disabilities, essential workers, and educators. Valid photo ID is required where applicable. Everyone else can choose the regular 5- or 10-visit package.
  • Other discounts: package prices are final, and no other discounts or offers can be added. The community discount is already included in the community discount packages.
  • Appointments: our cancellation policy still applies to each scheduled visit, including late cancellation and no-show fees.

If you stop early

You can stop your package at any time. If you stop before using all your visits, the package discount no longer applies. The visits you've already had are recalculated at the regular follow-up price, and we refund the rest of what you paid. The regular price is $80 per visit, or $75 per visit with a community discount package.
