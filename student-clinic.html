Student Clinic

Affordable acupuncture and Oriental medicine care from supervised graduate interns, with every treatment plan reviewed by a licensed OMD.

At a Glance

Supervised intern care with more time, lower cost, and OMD oversight.

Our student clinic is where graduate students at Wongu University gain hands-on clinical experience treating real patients under the direct supervision of faculty supervisors — all highly trained acupuncturists and herbalists.

As a university teaching clinic, our student interns provide care under licensed OMD supervision. Every diagnosis is reviewed and every treatment plan is approved before treatment begins.

Visit Flow

What to Expect at Your Student Clinic Visit

The process is simple, supervised, and designed to give your intern enough time to be thorough.

Book Your Appointment

Book online through our patient booking portal, or call (702) 852-1280. If you'd like an intern, mention the Student Clinic when you book. VA Community Care and Culinary Health Fund patients should contact Wongu Health Center before scheduling so we can verify eligibility, authorization, and provider availability. No medical intake forms or health records are collected on this website — your full health history is taken in person at your appointment.

Meet Your Intern & Supervisor

Your intern will conduct a thorough health intake, including traditional diagnostic methods. Their supervising OMD will review the findings and approve the treatment plan.

Receive Your Treatment

Your intern performs the acupuncture (and any additional modalities) under direct OMD oversight. Initial intern visits typically run 1.5 to 2 hours, while follow-up sessions are about 1 hour — giving your intern time to be thorough and attentive.

Follow-Up & Plan

After your session, your intern and supervisor will discuss next steps, answer questions, and recommend a treatment frequency tailored to your needs and goals.

Safety & Quality

How Student Clinic Care Is Supervised

Every student treatment is reviewed and supervised by a licensed Oriental Medicine Doctor. Here are the safeguards built into our teaching clinic.

Direct OMD Supervision

A licensed Oriental Medicine Doctor is physically present and directly supervises every treatment. They review all diagnoses and approve all treatment plans before any needles are placed.

Rigorous Academic Training

Our interns have completed hundreds of hours of coursework in anatomy, acupuncture theory, Chinese herbal medicine, and clinical safety before ever treating patients.

Sterile, Modern Facility

We use single-use, sterile, disposable needles and follow clean-needle technique, sanitation, and infection-control protocols.

Patient-Centered Approach

Interns are trained to be thorough, communicative, and gentle. Because they're learning, they often take more time and care than practitioners in busy private practices.

Beyond Patient Care

Building Southern Nevada's Healthcare Workforce

Our student clinic is more than affordable care — it's an investment in the future of Oriental medicine in Southern Nevada. Every patient visit helps train the next generation of acupuncture and herbal medicine practitioners under licensed OMD supervision.

The program also supports a scholarship pathway to increase the number of qualified practitioners serving our community, expanding both clinical capacity and access to care for underserved populations across the Las Vegas Valley.
