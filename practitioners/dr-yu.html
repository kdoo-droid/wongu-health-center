Care with Dr. Yu

As Clinic Director, Dr. Yu oversees the quality of care across Wongu Health Center, including the student clinic, where senior interns treat patients under licensed OMD supervision. She also sees patients directly.

Dr. Yu's experience centers on fertility and women's health and on long-standing chronic conditions that benefit from steady, whole-person care. Treatment may combine acupuncture with a custom herbal formula and practical guidance on sleep, diet, and daily habits.

Dr. Yu and Dr. Sekine also offer structured multi-visit treatment packages, including care for pregnancy and birth. If you are pregnant or trying to conceive, tell the front desk when you book, and keep your OB, midwife, or fertility specialist informed about your acupuncture care.

Credentials explained

  • MSOM: Master of Science in Oriental Medicine.
  • L.Ac.: Licensed Acupuncturist.
  • Dipl. OM: Diplomate of Oriental Medicine, national board certification (NCCAOM) in acupuncture and Chinese herbology.

Visit fees with a licensed OMD

A first visit with Dr. Yu is $120 (about an hour, consultation and treatment included). Follow-ups are $80. A standalone consultation without acupuncture is $100, and custom herbal formulas start at $15 per day. See full pricing.

Book with Dr. Yu

Request Dr. Yu when you book online, or call and our front desk will find a time. VA Community Care and Culinary patients should contact Wongu Health Center before scheduling so we can verify eligibility, authorization, and provider availability.
