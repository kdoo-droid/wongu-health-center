Care with Dr. Sekine

Dr. Sekine treats patients directly, and as a clinical supervisor he oversees senior interns in Wongu's student clinic. He reviews their assessments and treatments so every intern visit meets the standard of a licensed doctor.

Dr. Sekine is known for Japanese-style acupuncture. Japanese acupuncture traditions generally emphasize a gentle touch, careful palpation of the abdomen and channels to guide point selection, and often fewer and shallower needles. Many people who are nervous about needles find this approach comfortable. His treatments may also include cupping and moxibustion (gentle heat from the herb mugwort) when they suit the patient.

Patients see Dr. Sekine for chronic internal conditions and for pain, including back pain.
