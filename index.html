Custom Herbal Formulas

Support Your Care Between Visits

Acupuncture is only one part of traditional Oriental medicine. When appropriate, our licensed OMDs can build a custom herbal formula — blended specifically for you, not an off-the-shelf product — along with herbal teas, pills, powders, or topical preparations to help support your treatment plan at home.

Our clinic carries a working herbal dispensary, so patients can ask about formulas during an appointment instead of trying to guess which herbs to buy on their own. Your practitioner will hand-select and combine herbs to match your needs, constitution, and current treatment goals.

Custom-Built for You

No two formulas are alike. Yours is selected and combined after your practitioner reviews your symptoms, tongue, pulse, and treatment goals.

Herbal Teas & Formulas

We offer teas, classical formulas, granules, pills, powders, tinctures, and topical options when available.

Tell your practitioner about all medications and supplements you take. Herbal formulas may interact with medications and may not be appropriate during pregnancy or for certain medical conditions.
