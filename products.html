About Jawoongo

A Classic Formula, Made by Our University

Jawoongo (자운고) is the Korean name for a classic herbal formula known in Chinese as Zi Yun Gao and in Japanese as Shiunko. The formula dates back centuries. Many traditional versions are a deep purple-red from Zi Cao (lithospermum root). Ours is lighter: a soft pinkish color, somewhere between light purple and yellow.

It has long been a household staple in Korea, Japan, and China, where families keep a jar on hand for dry, rough, or chapped skin. Ours is made from natural ingredients right here at Wongu University of Oriental Medicine.

Ingredients

The roller and the jar are made from the same herbal formula:

  • Grapeseed oil
  • Dang Gui (Chinese angelica root)
  • Zi Cao (lithospermum root)
  • Bai Shao (white peony root)
  • Bai Xian Pi (dictamnus root bark)
  • Bai Zhi (Angelica dahurica root)
  • Gan Cao (licorice root)
  • Huang Qi (astragalus root)
  • Huang Qin (Baikal skullcap root)
  • Ku Shen (sophora root)
  • Lian Qiao (forsythia fruit)
  • Qing Pi (immature tangerine peel)
  • Zhi Shi (immature bitter orange)
  • Ju Hua (chrysanthemum flower)

The jar also contains beeswax, which gives it its balm texture. The roller has no beeswax.

Good to Know

Using Jawoongo

  • A little goes a long way. Apply a thin layer to clean, dry skin.
  • Apply carefully around clothing. The oil base, the beeswax in the jar, and the natural herbal color can leave marks on clothing, sheets, and towels. Apply it with care and let it soak in before getting dressed. It washes off skin with soap and water.
  • Try a small patch first, especially if you have sensitive skin or allergies. Jawoongo contains chrysanthemum, so take extra care if you're allergic to ragweed, daisies, or similar plants.
  • Be mindful of the sun. A few of the herbs (Bai Zhi and the citrus peels) can make skin more sensitive to sunlight. It's best used at night or on skin that will be covered.
  • Store it somewhere cool. Natural oils and balms can soften or spoil in a hot car, which is easy to do in a Las Vegas summer.

For external use only. Do not use on deep, infected, or severe wounds or burns. Stop using it if irritation or a rash develops. Keep out of reach of children. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or have a skin condition, ask your practitioner or doctor before use. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
