How to Use

Simple as One, Two, Three

  1. Start with clean, dry skin. Wash and pat the area dry.
  2. Apply a thin layer. A little goes a long way. Roll it on, or warm a small amount of balm between your fingers.
  3. Let it soak in. Give it a few minutes before getting dressed, since the oils can mark fabric.
About Jawoongo

A Classic Formula, Made by Our University

Jawoongo (자운고) is the Korean name for a classic herbal formula known in Chinese as Zi Yun Gao and in Japanese as Shiunko. It has been a household staple in Korea, Japan, and China for centuries, where families keep it on hand for dry, rough, or chapped skin.

Many traditional versions are a deep purple-red from Zi Cao (lithospermum root). Ours is lighter: a soft pinkish color, somewhere between light purple and yellow. It's made from natural ingredients right here at Wongu University of Oriental Medicine.

Ingredients

The roller and the jar are made from the same herbal formula:

  • Grapeseed oil
  • Dang Gui (Chinese angelica root)
  • Zi Cao (lithospermum root)
  • Bai Shao (white peony root)
  • Bai Xian Pi (dictamnus root bark)
  • Bai Zhi (Angelica dahurica root)
  • Gan Cao (licorice root)
  • Huang Qi (astragalus root)
  • Huang Qin (Baikal skullcap root)
  • Ku Shen (sophora root)
  • Lian Qiao (forsythia fruit)
  • Qing Pi (immature tangerine peel)
  • Zhi Shi (immature bitter orange)
  • Ju Hua (chrysanthemum flower)

The jar also contains beeswax, which gives it its balm texture. The roller has no beeswax.
